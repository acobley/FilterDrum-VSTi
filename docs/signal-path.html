FilterDrum · monophonic MS-20 drum voice

Signal path

One mono voice, drawn as the code actually runs it. The thing worth noticing first: the two excitations do not meet at a summing node. The noise goes into the filter's input; the trigger ping is written straight into the filter's output integrator. They enter at different points, which is why the resonance knob changes one of them far more than the other.

What updates when

Most questions about why two hits differ come down to this table. Three different clocks are running, and a value read on the wrong one is the usual cause.

Filter cutoff every sample The envelope sweep is smooth; a per-block cutoff would zipper on a fast kick.
Both AR envelopes every sample… …but only while the VCA envelope is not idle. This is the gate that freezes the VCF envelope.

the one known defect

renderVoices returns early while the VCA envelope is idle, and both envelopes are advanced inside that loop. A VCF release longer than the VCA's therefore never completes — it freezes mid-release, and the next hit starts its attack from that frozen level instead of from zero.

Measured hit 2 against hit 1, noise off: +0.00 dB at the defaults (VCF 120 ms / VCA 150 ms), −1.27 dB at 1000/100, and −6.60 dB at 4000/50. The first note of a session is a different sound from every note after it.
