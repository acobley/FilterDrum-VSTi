Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. FilterDrum appears as an instrument by AE Cobley.

Four things that are deliberate, and are the ones most likely to look like faults:

  • Noise Level at 0 is silence, not a quiet tone. The noise is the voice’s only excitation — there is no oscillator — and a filter fed exact zero from a zero state stays at exact zero however high the resonance.
  • Note‑off is ignored. The envelopes are triggered, not gated, so a hit sounds the same whether the key was tapped or held. The Release controls decide how long it rings.
  • Run arms the sequencer. It starts on the next line the Launch On division fires — bar, ½, ¼, ⅛ or 1/16 — so it can wait most of a bar before the first step. With the transport stopped it waits indefinitely, which is correct and looks like nothing happening.
  • Sequenced steps play at full velocity. There is no per-step level, so the four Velocity controls affect incoming MIDI only.

Known gap: a VCF release set longer than its VCA release does not finish — the voice stops advancing once the amplitude envelope is done. It is worth about 0.4 dB and is documented rather than hidden.
